About Macon County

Welcome to Macon County, North Carolina!

Located in the beautiful Blue Ridge Mountains. Macon County is known for its breathtaking views and rich history.

Our History

Macon County was founded in 1828 and includes Franklin, Highlands, Nantahala, and Otto.

While Macon County has grown immensely since then, we have always been dedicated to making our home and our constituents happy.

What We Have to Offer

Need something to do? We've got you covered! If you love to hike, fish, camp, or kayak, you've come to the right place.

With beautiful open skies, you get a breathtaking view of the stars.
